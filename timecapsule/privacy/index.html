Privacy Policy

This policy explains how QwerNFC TimeCapsule handles Google account information, event details and guest photographs when the relevant service features are enabled.

1. Who operates the service

QwerNFC TimeCapsule is an event-photo capture and gallery service. For privacy questions or data requests, email qwernfctc99@gmail.com.

2. Information involved

  • Event details, such as event name, type, dates and organizer information supplied for an event.
  • For Google sign-in or Drive authorization: Google account identifier, verified email address where provided, authorization state, and limited OAuth credentials needed to access the event's authorized files.
  • Photos submitted by event guests and basic photo/session metadata, such as submission time, session identifiers, upload status, and review decisions.
  • Basic technical and security logs needed to operate and protect the website and API.

3. How Google user data is accessed and used

An event owner voluntarily authorizes Google Drive access through Google's own consent screen. The requested drive.file permission allows the app to work with files it creates or that the user explicitly opens or shares with the app; it is not a request to browse every private Drive file. When the owner connects, TimeCapsule can create a dedicated event folder, upload event photographs and retrieve approved photos for the event gallery.

Google authorization credentials are intended for backend-only use. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, and guest browsers do not receive Google access or refresh tokens. The service does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence or for advertising and does not sell Google user data.

4. Event photographs and publication

Submitted photographs are stored in the connected event owner's Google Drive. The TimeCapsule service keeps event and photo metadata needed to manage submissions, review images and maintain the gallery. Photos should not appear in the public event gallery until an authorized manager approves them and enables publication.

Approved photos may be viewable by people with the event gallery link. Event organizers and guests should not upload material without the necessary consent and permissions, particularly images of children or other sensitive situations.

5. Service providers and sharing

Google provides owner-authorized Drive storage and sign-in. Cloudflare provides application hosting and database infrastructure. GitHub Pages serves public informational pages. Information may be processed by those providers as needed for these functions. We do not share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention, disconnection and deletion

Event photos remain in the event owner's Google Drive unless the owner deletes or moves them; disconnecting TimeCapsule does not by itself delete the owner's Drive files. Event metadata and encrypted credentials are retained while needed for the event service and operational or security purposes. Deletion and disconnection requests can be submitted to the support email above and will be handled according to applicable requirements. We do not promise immediate erasure of all provider backups.

Owners can also revoke the TimeCapsule connection using Google Account connected-app settings. Revoking access stops new authorized requests but may leave existing event photos in the owner's Drive.

7. Security and limitations

TimeCapsule uses event-scoped authorization, encrypted OAuth credentials and access checks designed to separate clients' files. No online service can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted long-term availability. Protect your Google account and keep an independent backup of important photographs.

8. Your choices

To ask about access, corrections, removal of an image, disconnection, or deletion of TimeCapsule-held data, contact qwernfctc99@gmail.com and identify the affected event without emailing passwords, private access tokens or unnecessary sensitive data.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this page as TimeCapsule features and applicable requirements change. The most recent update date is shown above.
